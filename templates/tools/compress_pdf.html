Tips to Reduce PDF Size Even Further

While the ToolHub pdf compressor does the heavy lifting automatically, there are a few strategies you can apply before or after compression to achieve the smallest possible file size.

Optimize Before You Create

When creating a PDF in Word, Google Docs, or a design application, choose lower image resolutions if the document is intended for screen viewing only. A seventy-two DPI image is perfectly adequate for on-screen reading and produces a much smaller file than a three hundred DPI version.

Flatten Transparency

PDFs with transparent layers and complex blending modes can be larger than necessary. Flattening transparency before export eliminates hidden complexity that some compression algorithms struggle to reduce.

Remove Unused Elements

Some PDFs contain hidden layers, annotations, metadata, and embedded files that the average reader never sees. Removing these elements before compression can lead to additional savings.

Choose the Right Compression Level

If you need to reduce pdf size to under 1mb, you may need to apply more aggressive compression to image-heavy documents. ToolHub's intelligent engine automatically balances quality and size, but for extreme cases you can re-run the process to achieve further reduction.

Split Very Large Documents

If a single PDF contains hundreds of pages and compression alone does not bring the size down enough, consider splitting the document into logical sections. ToolHub's companion Split PDF tool makes this straightforward.
