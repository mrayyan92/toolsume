Tips for Deleting Pages from a PDF Effectively

Follow these tips to get the best results when you delete pages from a PDF using ToolHub.

Preview your document first. Before entering page numbers, open the PDF in a viewer and note the exact page numbers you want to remove. This prevents accidental deletion of important pages and saves you from re-uploading the file.

Double-check your page ranges. A common mistake is entering a range like 3-7 when you meant 3-5. Verify the numbers before clicking Delete Pages to avoid unintended removals.

Use commas for non-sequential pages. If you need to remove pages 2, 8, 15, and 20, enter them as 2,8,15,20 rather than trying to create multiple ranges. The comma format is cleaner and less error-prone.

Keep a backup of the original file. Although ToolHub processes your document securely, it is always a good practice to keep the original PDF saved on your device. That way, you can re-upload and reprocess it if you make a mistake.

Process large files in batches. If your PDF has hundreds of pages and you need to delete many of them, consider breaking the task into smaller batches. This ensures reliable processing and gives you more control over the final output.

Check the download before sharing. After downloading the new PDF, open it and scroll through the pages to confirm that the correct pages were removed and that no content was lost or rearranged.
