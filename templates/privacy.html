PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolHub may handle information when you use our website and online tools.

Last updated: {% block privacy_date %}{{ privacy_date|default("June 2026") }}{% endblock %}
This policy is written for the current ToolHub website and is intended to clearly explain our general privacy practices. It is not legal advice. Update this policy if your data collection, analytics, advertising, email services, or file-processing infrastructure changes.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use ToolHub, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as information submitted through a contact form or other communication with us.

ToolHub may also receive technical information associated with normal website use, such as browser type, device information, approximate usage information, pages visited, and similar information needed to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.

2. Files You Upload

Some ToolHub features require you to upload a file so that a requested operation can be performed. The type of file and the processing method depend on the particular tool.

You should not upload confidential, highly sensitive, or legally protected information unless you are comfortable with the way that particular ToolHub tool processes the file.

File handling, temporary storage, processing, and deletion can vary between tools. We will document specific practices where they apply.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • provide and operate ToolHub tools;
  • respond to questions, feedback, or support requests;
  • maintain website security and prevent abuse;
  • diagnose errors and improve performance;
  • understand general website usage and improve the user experience; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ToolHub may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security, preferences, analytics, or other features.

If third-party analytics or advertising services are added to ToolHub, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their respective policies.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

ToolHub may display advertising in the future, including advertising provided by third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense.

Where advertising services are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising as permitted by their policies and applicable law.

Any advertising-related disclosures and consent mechanisms required for the visitors and regions served by ToolHub should be implemented before advertising is enabled.

6. Third-Party Services

ToolHub may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, email delivery, advertising, file processing, or other website functionality. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

We do not use this section to claim that a particular third-party service is currently active. The actual list of providers should be updated as services are added to the live website.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information and the website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to operate and secure the service, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or for other legitimate purposes.

Retention periods for uploaded files can vary by tool and technical implementation. Specific retention information should be provided when a tool has a defined file-retention period.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, which can include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of certain information.

If you have a privacy-related request, contact ToolHub through the Contact page and provide enough information for us to understand and respond to your request.

10. Children's Privacy

ToolHub is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ToolHub's features, services, technologies, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ToolHub's privacy practices, please use our Contact page.
